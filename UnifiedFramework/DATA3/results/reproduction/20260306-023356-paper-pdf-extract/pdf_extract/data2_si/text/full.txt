       Zeta Potential Measurements

       Three trials using 2 cm × 1 cm squares of membrane were conducted. The samples were
       first soaked in deionized water overnight. The samples were placed parallel to one another,
       leaving a small gap between them, creating a rectangular channel for the electrolyte solution
1260   to pass through. A 1 mM KCl solution at pH 2 is used to flush the space between the samples.
       Afterwards, a select volume of 1 mM KCl at pH 2 is passed through the rectangular channel.


                                                                       SI-7
       The pressure is ramped from 600 mbar to 200 mbar and the voltage along the pressure
       ramp is recorded. Using voltage, pressure, conductivity, and viscosity of the solution, a zeta
       potential is calculated. This sequence is repeated three times at pH values ranging from 2
1265   to 11.


       Surface Charge Density and Partition Coeﬃcient Analysis

       Figure S9 depicts the zeta potential measurements for an NF270 membrane. Diafiltration
       experiments were conducted at the unadjusted pH of the KCl solutions (∼pH 6). Taking
       the zeta potential measurement at pH 6, the Gouy-Chapman equation 86 can be used to
1270   calculate the surface charge density. Assuming the charge is uniformly distributed within
       the void volume of the pores, an eﬀective volumetric charge density (χ) of 140 mM was
       calculated for the NF270 membranes.

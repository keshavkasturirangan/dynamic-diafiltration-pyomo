   The results in Table 6 underscore the significant role of
modeling the startup processes. Lag mode experiments that
model startup (i.e, M1) are the most informative. Overflow
experiments or lag experiments that do not model startup
processes (e.g., prior work26) provide comparable amounts of
information. As such, accurately capturing the early stage
dynamics of lag experiments allows for more precise parameter
estimation, ultimately leading to a better understanding of solute
transport mechanisms. This information gain exemplifies the
synergistic feedback between experimental design, model
development, and data analysis, driving more informed and
efficient advancements in membrane characterization.
